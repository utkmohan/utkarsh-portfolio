04 Photography

Moments Through
my Lens

Photography, to me, is a way of slowing down in a fast world , capturing the candid, the quiet, and the chaotic. From spontaneous street shots to campus life, and from wild nature trails to still indoor moods, I try to frame stories that speak without words.

This space is a growing archive of the people, places, and perspectives I’ve encountered - on and off campus, across continents, or just in everyday light.
